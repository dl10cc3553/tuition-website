About H.S. Gupta

Combining engineering insights with pedagogical excellence to nurture future scientific minds.

H.S. Gupta is a highly experienced educator and retired Senior Superintending Engineer from the prestigious Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), New Delhi, with more than 20 years of experience in teaching and technical mentoring.


He specialises in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) for Classes 6 to 10, combining strong conceptual clarity with real-world engineering insight. His unique teaching methodology builds a solid academic foundation, develops analytical problem-solving skills, and equips students with the confidence required to excel in school examinations, competitive Olympiads, and future higher-secondary studies.
